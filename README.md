# IT326Project
# Daily Stress Level Prediction Using Wearable and Lifestyle Data

## Problem

Stress is a common problem that many people experience in their daily lives, yet they may not be aware of how everyday lifestyle factors such as sleep, physical activity, diet, and caffeine intake are associated with their stress levels. Understanding the relationship between these factors and daily stress levels can help identify which aspects of an individual's lifestyle may be linked to higher or lower levels of stress.

## Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

- Identify patterns and relationships between lifestyle factors, such as sleep, physical activity, diet, and caffeine intake, and daily stress levels.
- Develop a classification model to predict daily stress levels as Low, Medium, or High based on wearable and lifestyle-related factors.
- Apply clustering techniques to discover groups of records with similar lifestyle and stress-related characteristics.
- Evaluate and compare the results of the classification and clustering techniques to identify meaningful patterns and outcomes in the dataset.

## Motivation

We chose this dataset because it provides an opportunity to examine daily stress levels through measurable lifestyle and wearable-related factors. Rather than relying on assumptions about what may influence stress, data mining allows us to analyze the available data and identify meaningful patterns within it. The combination of classification and clustering also allows us to approach the problem from different perspectives, both by predicting stress levels and by discovering groups of records with similar characteristics.
